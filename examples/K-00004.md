SYMPTOM: the humidity series lost between one and three readings at the top of most hours, while
temperature from the same frames was complete. Every missing reading was in the last minute
before the hour.
CAUSE: log rotation renamed the CSV out from under the writer, which kept its file handle open
and continued appending to the rotated file until its buffer flushed on the next write. The
readings were never lost — they were written to yesterday's file, after the summary job had
already read it.
FIX: the writer reopens by name after rotation, and the summary job reads the rotated file once
more, after a delay longer than the flush interval.
VERIFIED: rotation triggered manually four times with a synthetic frame in flight; all four
frames appear in the correct file. Re-checked against a week of production data: no gaps.
