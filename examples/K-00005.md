WHEN: the poll interval was shortened from one frame every ten seconds to one frame per second,
to make the humidity curve smoother in the daily plot. No other change; same unit, same cable.
THEN: 214 of 600 frames failed their checksum, in bursts rather than uniformly, and the bursts
line up with the unit's own transmit windows. Reverting the interval restored a clean link
within one polling cycle. The smoother curve was never obtained; what was obtained was a curve
with a third of its points silently dropped by the parser, which looked smoother.
