The outdoor unit reports temperature and humidity as integers in TENTHS of a unit, with no
decimal point and no unit suffix: the frame "T=+0213 H=0561 B=87" means 21.3 °C, 56.1 % relative
humidity, 87 % battery. Battery is whole percent, i.e. a different scale in the same frame.
Frame timestamps are assigned by the logger on receipt, in UTC; the outdoor unit itself sends no
time of its own.
