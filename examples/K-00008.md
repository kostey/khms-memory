RULE: poll the outdoor unit once per second, so that short weather events are not missed and the
daily plot is smooth.
LIMITS: assumes the serial link carries the traffic; not measured at the time this was written.
IMPLICATIONS: the summary job can take the first reading of each minute and ignore the rest.

(This card is kept, marked superseded, because it is the reason the 1 Hz attempt was made at
all — see K-00005 for what happened and K-00009 for what replaced it. Deleting it would leave
the next reader free to have the same good idea again.)
